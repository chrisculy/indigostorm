# Indigo Storm — Beat Sheet (Draft)

## Premise

A woman wakes in a locked clinic with no memory, the reflexes of a trained killer, and a team already on its way to "collect" her. Over thirty days she pieces together who she was, discovers she helped build the program now hunting her, and races to destroy it before it can be switched on.

## The secret (the spine the reveals hang on)

**INDIGO STORM** is a black-budget neuro-conditioning program run by **Adrian Holt** behind a defense contractor. It was sold to its recruits as a way to create undetectable deep-cover agents. Its real purpose is the *Storm*: a single remote activation signal that will turn dozens of conditioned sleepers, already embedded in security services across Europe, into Holt's weapons on command. The activation runs from a master system called **the Source**, held at a black site called **MERIDIAN**.

The protagonist was one of the program's best assets. She discovered the Storm, tried to leak it, and was caught. Her handler wiped her memory to save her life—and hid the Source's kill-code inside her conditioning.

## Cast

- **Nadia Kerr** (codename KESTREL, "Asset Seven") — the protagonist. Former military intelligence officer and volunteer to the program.
- **Tobias Reyne** — her former handler. Ally, guide on the comms, and the man who wiped her. Captured at the end of Act II.
- **Adrian Holt** — the Director of Indigo Storm. The antagonist.
- **CORVID** ("Asset Four") — another conditioned operative and Holt's lead hunter. Nadia's mirror; turned to her side in Act III.
- **Lucien Abara** — a black-market fixer. Her first Ally (Day 6).

## Act summaries

**Act I — Amnesia & Extraction (Days 1–10).** Nadia escapes the clinic, follows the clues she left herself, and learns the name *Indigo Storm*. She ends the act finding Tobias Reyne alive, and he offers her a way inside. *Theme: who am I?*

**Act II — Inside the Program (Days 11–20).** With Tobias's help, Nadia goes on the offensive: hacking the program, raiding its facilities, and meeting CORVID. The midpoint flashback reveals she volunteered. The second reveals she tried to stop the Storm once before. The act ends with Holt framing her for an atrocity: a global burn notice, and Tobias taken. *Theme: what did I do?*

**Act III — Burn Notice (Days 21–30).** Hunted by everyone, Nadia learns she is the key to the Source. She turns CORVID, storms MERIDIAN, frees Tobias, and redacts the Source before the activation completes. *Theme: what will I do now?*

## Day by day

### Act I — Amnesia & Extraction

**Day 1 — Cold Open** · 🥷 Stealth · *Base rules*
Nadia wakes in a sterile private clinic in Rotterdam with no memory and a guard outside her door. She slips out through the service corridors with only a hospital gown and a sense she has done this before.
*Hook:* Written on her palm in pen is a single word: KESTREL.

**Day 2 — Pocket Litter** · 👁️ Recon
Hiding in a dockside storage unit, she goes through the clinic records she grabbed: her intake form is blank except for a billing code and a Zurich bank reference.
*Hook:* The clinic's records show she was admitted voluntarily—three days ago, by a "T. Reyne."

**Day 3 — The Cleaners** · ⚡ Force · *Perk unlock*
A professional team arrives to "collect" her. Her body knows how to fight before her mind catches up.
*Hook:* One of the cleaners, dying, says: "They'll send Four next."

**Day 4 — Rush Hour** · 🥷 Stealth · *Twist: Crowded station*
At Brussels-Midi she disappears into the morning crowd to shake facial-recognition cameras and a tail on her way south.
*Hook:* On a departures screen, her own face appears for a split second, tagged MISSING PATIENT – DANGEROUS.

**Day 5 — Safe Deposit** · 👁️ Recon
In a Zurich bank vault she finds passports in six names, cash, and a recorded message from herself: "Don't trust the program. Find Tobias Reyne. He owes us."
*Hook:* The vault's security log shows someone else opened the same box an hour earlier.

**Day 6 — The Fixer** · ⚡ Force · *New rules: Gear & Allies*
To get equipped and get a lead on Reyne, she goes to Lucien Abara, a fixer who seems to know her. The deal goes loud.
*Hook:* Lucien gives her Reyne's last known address, then admits Reyne paid him to wait for her.

**Day 7 — Reyne's Apartment** · 🥷 Stealth · *Perk unlock*
She breaks into Reyne's abandoned Geneva apartment, which is watched by both police and program surveillance.
*Hook:* The walls are covered with a timeline of her life, with every photo's face scratched out except hers.

**Day 8 — Night Watch** · 👁️ Recon · *Twist: Night op*
She stakes out a program courier making a night drop at a lakeside marina and hears the phrase "Indigo Storm" for the first time.
*Hook:* The courier's case is stamped with the same billing code as her clinic intake form.

**Day 9 — Rooftops** · ⚡ Force
A rooftop chase through Geneva's old town to take the courier's drive before it reaches a handoff.
*Hook:* The drive holds one file: a countdown clock with no label, 21 days from zero.

**Day 10 — Extraction** · 🥷 Stealth · *Perk unlock · Act I Debrief*
Following the drive's metadata, she finds Tobias Reyne alive, hiding in a mountain safehouse, and has to slip through a program cordon to reach him.
*Hook:* Tobias opens the door, sees her, and says, "You came back. Then you don't remember what I did."

### Act II — Inside the Program

**Day 11 — Firewall** · 👁️ Recon · *New rules: Encrypted Threats*
From Tobias's safehouse she breaches the program's network, layer by encrypted layer, and learns Indigo Storm is not a single operation but an *activation*.
*Hook:* A list of names on the network includes hers: Asset Seven. Status: WIPED – RECOVER.

**Day 12 — Cold Storage** · ⚡ Force
A raid on a program storage facility in an old Swiss army bunker to seize conditioning records before they're destroyed.
*Hook:* The records show the program has 41 active sleepers, embedded in security services across Europe.

**Day 13 — Radio Silence** · 🥷 Stealth · *Perk unlock · Twist: Comms blackout*
She infiltrates Holt's defense-contractor headquarters in Berlin with Tobias's comms jammed and no support.
*Hook:* In Holt's office is a framed photo of the program's first graduating class, and she's standing in the front row.

**Day 14 — Asset Seven** · 👁️ Recon
She decrypts her own personnel file: service record, psychological profile, and a mission log that ends abruptly.
*Hook:* Her final mission is listed as "Internal — Source integrity," assigned by Holt himself.

**Day 15 — Corvid** · ⚡ Force
Her first fight with CORVID, Asset Four, a conditioned operative who fights exactly like her and seems to know her.
*Hook:* As he escapes, CORVID says, "You used to say the conditioning didn't hurt. Do you still believe that?"

**Day 16 — The Volunteer** · 🥷 Stealth · *New rules: Memory Flashbacks · Flashback 1*
Sneaking back into the Berlin facility to recover CORVID's file, she passes through the conditioning wing, and a memory surfaces.
*Flashback:* She wasn't taken. She volunteered, believing the program would save lives.
*Hook:* If she built this, what else did she do?

**Day 17 — The Director** · 👁️ Recon · *Perk unlock*
She surveils Adrian Holt at a defense-industry gala, mapping his security and listening to his conversations.
*Hook:* Holt confirms the countdown is the Storm's activation date, and the Source is "back at MERIDIAN, where it belongs."

**Day 18 — Convoy** · ⚡ Force · *Twist: Handler on the line*
With Tobias guiding her over the comms, she ambushes the armored convoy carrying an activation keycard to MERIDIAN.
*Hook:* The keycard is coded to her biometrics.

**Day 19 — The Leak** · 🥷 Stealth · *Flashback 2*
She slips into the program's archive to learn why the keycard knows her, and a second memory breaks loose.
*Flashback:* Months ago she discovered what the Storm really was and tried to leak it to the press. Tobias caught her.
*Hook:* Why is Tobias still helping her?

**Day 20 — Burn Notice** · 👁️ Recon · *Perk unlock · Act II Debrief*
While she's mining Holt's servers for the Source's location, Holt moves first: a bombing in Berlin is pinned on her, and a global burn notice goes out.
*Hook:* The safehouse goes dark. When she gets back, Tobias is gone, and his earpiece is left on the table for her to find.

### Act III — Burn Notice

**Day 21 — Hunted** · ⚡ Force · *New rules: Pursuers*
Every agency in Europe is after her, and CORVID's hunter-killer team is closing in. She has to break out of the city.
*Hook:* CORVID's team doesn't try to kill her. They try to take her alive.

**Day 22 — The Key** · 🥷 Stealth · *Flashback 3*
In a safe house Tobias prepared months ago, she finds his recorded confession, and the final memory returns.
*Flashback:* Holt ordered her executed. Tobias wiped her instead to save her life, and hid the Source's kill-code inside her conditioning.
*Hook:* She is the key. That's why Holt wants her alive.

**Day 23 — Dead Drop** · 👁️ Recon
She works through Tobias's last dead drop to find the location of MERIDIAN: a decommissioned radar station in the Norwegian Arctic.
*Hook:* MERIDIAN's prisoner manifest lists one new arrival: T. Reyne.

**Day 24 — Lockdown** · ⚡ Force · *Perk unlock · Twist: Lockdown*
The city goes into security lockdown around her, and she has to break through the checkpoints to reach a northbound transport.
*Hook:* At the last checkpoint, CORVID is waiting, alone.

**Day 25 — Turning Corvid** · 🥷 Stealth
Instead of fighting, she gets close enough to use what Tobias taught her and breaks CORVID's conditioning.
*Hook:* CORVID remembers who he was, and he knows the way into MERIDIAN.

**Day 26 — Meridian** · 👁️ Recon · *New rules: Black-Site Lockdown (Two-Phase Threats)*
With CORVID's help she maps and infiltrates the black site, but every system she beats triggers a second layer of defense.
*Hook:* The Source is already live, and the countdown is down to its final hours.

**Day 27 — Countermeasures** · ⚡ Force
She fights through MERIDIAN's internal security to the detention level and frees Tobias.
*Hook:* Tobias is barely conscious. "The kill-code only works from the core, and only if you remember all of it."

**Day 28 — Alarm Raised** · 🥷 Stealth · *Twist: Alarm raised*
The whole base is hunting them. She has to reach the core while CORVID and Tobias draw the guards away.
*Hook:* Holt is waiting at the core, and he has started the activation early.

**Day 29 — The Source** · 👁️ Recon
A race to decrypt the Source and enter the kill-code from memory while sleepers across Europe begin to wake.
*Hook:* The code needs one last input: her own conditioning key. Entering it will wipe her memory again.

**Day 30 — Redact the Source** · ⚡ Force · *Epilogue*
The final confrontation with Holt in the Source chamber. Nadia must stop the activation and destroy the Source for good.
*Epilogue tiers (by campaign Stars):* Low: the Storm is stopped but Holt escapes, and Nadia loses her memories again. Middle: the Storm is stopped, Holt is captured, and Nadia keeps her memories. High: the Storm is stopped, the program is exposed to the world, Holt faces trial, and Nadia chooses who she wants to be.

## Act Debrief tiers (flavor only)

- **Day 10:** How cleanly Nadia reached Tobias. Low: barely, wounded and followed. Middle: clean. High: clean, with a lead on Holt already in hand.
- **Day 20:** How much of Holt's network she exposed before the burn notice. This shapes how the press covers the bombing, not what happens next.
